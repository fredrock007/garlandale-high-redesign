Frequently asked questions.

Helpful starting points for families exploring admission.

How do I apply for Grade 8?

Start with the official WCED admissions information. Confirm the relevant application period and procedure before applying.

What documents might I need?

Use the preparation checklist above as a starting point. Check WCED’s current document requirements, including any alternatives that apply to your circumstances.

Can I apply for a transfer?

Contact WCED and the school for guidance on the relevant grade, application procedure and availability. A place is not guaranteed.

What are the school fees?

Current fees and any applicable exemption procedures have not been verified for this concept. Contact the school for official information.

Who can I contact for admissions help?

Use the school contact page or official WCED channels for guidance. This independent website concept does not accept applications.
