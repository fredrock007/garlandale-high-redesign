1982 — Today

From Athlone 2 to Garlandale High.

Garlandale High opened in 1982 under the working name Athlone 2. Reporting on the school's history states that it received its Garlandale name the following year.

This independent concept would give the school a platform to share its own voice, leadership, history and values following approval.
